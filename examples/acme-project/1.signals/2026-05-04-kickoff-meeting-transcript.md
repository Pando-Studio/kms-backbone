# Kickoff meeting — Acme Travel booking flow (condensed transcript)

> Raw signal. Condensed from a 55-minute call. Verbatim quotes kept where they matter; no interpretation added.

**Lena** — Thanks for making time. We'd like to hear the problems in your words before we look at any data.

**Marta** — The short version: people find trips, they start booking, they don't finish. Especially on phones. Our booking flow was designed in 2021 for desktop and we've bolted things onto it ever since. "On mobile it feels like filling in a tax form."

**Tom** — Where do you see them give up?

**Marta** — Mostly at payment. But honestly the whole thing is five screens of forms before you even see the pay button. Traveler details, extras, insurance upsell, then payment, then confirmation.

**Ben** — From the support side, payment is the loudest. We get tickets every week saying "my card was refused but I was charged" or "the page froze after I clicked pay". Often it's a 3-D Secure redirect that loses people on mobile — they come back from their banking app and the session is gone. They have to start over and most just leave.

**Lena** — Do you have numbers on that?

**Ben** — Support tags, yes. Payment-step tickets are our biggest category, ahead of cancellations. I can export the last quarter.

**Marta** — And analytics — Priya on our side can pull the funnel. I know mobile converts far worse than desktop but I don't have the exact figures in my head. N/C on precise numbers today.

**Tom** — Anything you'd want to add, beyond fixing the drop-off?

**Marta** — Wishes, in rough order: guest checkout — right now you must create an account to book, and we suspect it kills bookings; saved traveler profiles for repeat customers; and Apple Pay / Google Pay. Also the insurance upsell screen — sales likes it, but I think it interrupts the flow at the worst moment.

**Ben** — And clearer error messages at payment. Today everything fails with "an error occurred". Support pays for that vagueness daily.

**Lena** — What's explicitly not on the table?

**Marta** — Search and the results page stay as they are — that part performs fine. And nothing that touches the inventory system; that's a separate contract with another vendor.

**Next steps** — Priya to send funnel export by 2026-05-06. Ben to send support-ticket breakdown. Acme Studio to come back with a problem synthesis before proposing anything.
